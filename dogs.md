# Dogs

The domestic dog (*Canis lupus familiaris*) was the first domesticated animal, descended from wolves at least 15,000 years ago.

## Traits
- Highly social, with a strong ability to read human cues
- Exceptional sense of smell, used in search, rescue, and detection work
- Hundreds of breeds bred for herding, hunting, guarding, and companionship

## Care
- Daily exercise and mental stimulation
- Balanced diet, regular vet visits, and vaccinations
- Consistent training and socialization

## Fun facts
- A dog's nose print is unique, like a human fingerprint
- Dogs can learn hundreds of words and gestures
