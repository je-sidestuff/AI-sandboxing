# The Turtles

Barnaby and Clementine were two painted turtles who lived on a sun-drenched log in the middle of Willow Creek. They had shared the same mossy perch for as long as either could remember, which, for turtles, was quite a long time.

"Do you think the world past the bend is as green as this bank?" Clementine asked one warm afternoon, stretching her neck toward the golden sunlight.

Barnaby blinked slowly, considering the question with the deep, unhurried gravity of someone who had never rushed a day in his life. "Only one way to find out," he said. "Though the current is swifter down there."

"Then we shall be swift, too," Clementine replied, sliding smoothly off the log with a gentle *plop*.

Barnaby followed with a dignified splash. Together, they paddled beneath the dappled shade of the weeping willows, letting the cool water carry them past dragonflies skimming the reeds and silvery minnows darting between river stones. Whenever the current tugged a little too eagerly, Barnaby nudged Clementine toward the calmer shallows, and whenever Barnaby paused to inspect a tempting patch of duckweed, Clementine waited patiently on a half-submerged rock.

Around the bend, the creek widened into a glassy pool ringed with wild irises and water lilies in full bloom. A flat, sun-baked boulder sat right at the center, warm and waiting.

Clementine pulled herself onto the rock and smiled. "Greener, and sunnier too."

Barnaby hauled his heavy shell up beside hers with a contented sigh. "A fine journey," he agreed, closing his eyes against the midday warmth. "And we have all afternoon to do absolutely nothing at all."

Side by side, the two turtles tucked their feet into their shells, letting the sun warm their backs as the creek hummed its quiet song around them.

*The End*
