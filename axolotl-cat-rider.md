# The Axolotl Who Rode a Cat

Pip was an axolotl who was tired of being carried everywhere in a bucket.

One morning, while the humans were away, Pip wriggled out of the tank and splashed onto the kitchen floor. Across the tiles sat Biscuit, a large orange cat who was washing a paw and plainly planning nothing.

"Excuse me," said Pip, gills fluttering. "I'd like to see the garden."

Biscuit looked down. "You're a fish."

"Salamander," said Pip. "And I'm wet, so I'd move quickly if I were you."

Biscuit sighed, which was how Pip got on. A small pink axolotl clung to the fur between the cat's shoulders and held on tight. The two set off through the cat flap, Biscuit padding along with great dignity, Pip leaning forward like a tiny knight on a very fluffy steed.

They crossed the lawn. They passed a suspicious snail and a very rude pigeon. Pip waved at everything. Biscuit pretended not to know him.

At the pond, Pip slid off and floated in the water, smiling his permanent smile. "Perfect," he said.

Biscuit sat at the edge and watched over him until the sun began to sink. Then Pip climbed back aboard, damp and happy, and the two rode home.

Nobody ever found out how the tank got so empty, or why the cat was always a little bit wet.

*The End*
