# Cat Behavior

Many cat behaviors that seem odd make sense once you remember cats are both small predators and potential prey.

## Common Behaviors

- **Kneading:** pressing their paws into soft surfaces is a holdover from kittenhood nursing and often signals contentment.
- **Head bunting:** rubbing their cheeks on people and objects deposits scent from facial glands, marking things as familiar.
- **Bringing "gifts":** dropping prey (or toys) near you reflects hunting instinct, and may be a way of sharing.
- **Sitting in boxes:** small enclosed spaces feel safe and help cats conserve body heat.
- **Zoomies:** sudden bursts of running, often at dusk or dawn, release pent-up energy. Cats are crepuscular, most active at twilight.

## Reading Body Language

| Signal | Usually Means |
| --- | --- |
| Tail straight up | Friendly greeting |
| Slow blink | Trust and relaxation |
| Ears flattened back | Fear or irritation |
| Puffed-up tail | Startled or defensive |
| Exposed belly | Comfortable, but not always an invitation to touch |

## Fun Fact

Adult cats rarely meow at each other. They mostly reserve meowing for communicating with humans.
