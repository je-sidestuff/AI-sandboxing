# Cats

Cats (*Felis catus*) have lived alongside humans for around 10,000 years, beginning when wildcats were drawn to grain stores by rodents.

## Traits

- **Agile hunters:** flexible spines and retractable claws make them excellent climbers and pouncers.
- **Sleepy:** most cats sleep 12–16 hours a day.
- **Expressive:** they communicate with purrs, meows, tail positions, and slow blinks.

## Care Basics

- A diet high in animal protein
- Fresh water and a clean litter box
- Regular vet visits
- Play and scratching posts

## Fun Fact

A group of cats is called a *clowder*.
