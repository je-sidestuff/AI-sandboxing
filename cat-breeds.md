# Cat Breeds

A quick look at a few popular domestic cat breeds.

## Breeds

- **Siamese:** vocal, social, and strikingly blue-eyed, with a pointed coat.
- **Maine Coon:** one of the largest domestic breeds, known for a shaggy coat and gentle nature.
- **Persian:** long, luxurious fur and a flat face; calm and best suited to indoor life.
- **Bengal:** a spotted, wild-looking coat with a very active, playful personality.
- **Sphynx:** nearly hairless, warm to the touch, and famously affectionate.

## Fun Fact

Most pet cats are not a specific breed at all. They are "domestic shorthairs," and they come in every color and pattern.
